Cebyfi Support

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Support stays inside the Cebyfi app under Settings. Cebyfi has no account. Posters, drafts, and the balance stay on the device.

Camera, photo library, and microphone are used only when you tap the matching button. The first Studio visit shows the system notification dialog. This version does not send notifications and does not ask for tracking permission.

A canceled or failed Gift Shop purchase adds nothing. Clear All Data removes projects, drafts, the balance, and the New User Gift Pack claimed flag from this device.
