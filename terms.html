What Cebyfi does

Cebyfi lets you place type in front of or behind a photo, add a sticker, and render one poster at a time. Posters, drafts, and purchase records stay in the app on this device. Cebyfi has no account, no cloud sync, and no social feed.

Gift Shop

The Gift Shop sells consumable Gift Packs and Treasure Chests at these prices: $0.99, $2.99, $9.99, $19.99, $29.99, $49.99, $69.99, and $99.99. The shop list shows the pack or chest name and the US dollar price. The amount included in a pack is shown on that pack’s detail page. The shop does not sell a subscription.

A purchase is completed only after Apple verifies the transaction. Canceling or a failed purchase adds nothing. A preview in the editor spends nothing. A confirmed render spends 1 from the balance on this device. Saving the same finished poster again does not spend again.

The first time Studio opens, Cebyfi shows the system notification dialog, then a system alert for a New User Gift Pack with one Claim button. Claiming adds 10. That offer is stored on this device and is not shown again unless you use Clear All Data.

Consumable purchases and the balance are stored on this device. They cannot be restored after you remove the app or use Clear All Data.
