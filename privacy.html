Cebyfi Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026. Cebyfi is a poster editor. It does not create an account and it does not upload your photos, drafts, or finished posters.

What Cebyfi does not collect

Cebyfi does not ask for tracking permission, does not run analytics, does not read an advertising identifier, and does not send photos, text, microphone audio, or purchase records to a Cebyfi server. There is no account and no cloud copy.

Data stored on this device

  • Poster projects you render, including headline, caption, layout, layer order, and style.
  • An unfinished draft, if you save one.
  • A local balance, the New User Gift Pack claimed flag, and verified Gift Pack or Treasure Chest transaction identifiers. The shop list does not show how many credits a pack includes.
  • Whether the notification prompt has already been shown.

Notifications

The first time Studio opens, Cebyfi asks for notification permission with the system dialog. This version does not schedule or send notifications. Render results stay on the screen you are looking at.

Camera, Photos, and Microphone

  • Camera is requested only when you tap Take Photo, to capture a poster subject.
  • Photo library reading is requested only when you tap Import from Photos.
  • Photo library adding is requested only when you tap Save to Photos.
  • Microphone is requested only when you tap voice input. Dictated words stay in the text field until you confirm them. Cebyfi does not keep an audio file.

If you deny one of these, the poster can continue with another path: import instead of camera, camera or text-only instead of import, keyboard instead of the microphone, and the in-app copy if saving to Photos is denied.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Cebyfi keeps the verified transaction identifier and the balance on this device. Consumable packs cannot be restored after the app is deleted or Clear All Data runs. A canceled or failed purchase adds nothing.

Demo posters

The Demos tab shows scripted sample posters marked Demo. They are built into the app. They are not your photos and they are not loaded from a network.

Other parties

Apple processes the permission dialogs and the Gift Shop payment. Cebyfi does not share your posters, drafts, or balance with any other party.

Clear All Data

Settings can erase projects, drafts, cached poster files, the balance, the New User Gift Pack claimed flag, and the on-device purchase record. System permission choices stay in iOS Settings.

Contact

Privacy questions stay in Settings inside the app. This page opens inside the app.
